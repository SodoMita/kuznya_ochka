OBJECTIVE

Robots building robots, fighting robots. Hostile machines march the convoy route toward your CORE (20 integrity). Destroy them to strip Iron, Copper and Silicon — there is no money, only matter. One circuit deck, nine blueprints, thirteen hostile classes, twenty-six relics, thirteen modules, two field abilities. Survive wave 12 to clear a sector and open the world route. Every sector also carries a bonus objective (top-right chip): sealed hull, capture quotas, blitz launches or zero unit losses — paid in bonus grid, matter and score. Runs auto-save and resume; score feeds the archive.

THE CIRCUIT DECK

You fight with a deck of cards, drawn Slay-the-Spire style. Your run starts with 10 cards; salvage caches add more. Each fabrication window is a turn: your old hand is discarded and you draw 5 (Autoloader: +1). Playing a card costs matter, not energy — your resources are the mana pool.

CIRCUIT BOARDS are the only way to build: select one and tap the field to print its unit — the board is consumed into the discard pile and cycles back on reshuffle. SUBROUTINES are one-shot skills, including hand-control circuits that discard weak cards or recycle cards from discard. FIRMWARE installs a sector-wide modifier and always exhausts. CORRUPTIONS are unplayable curse cards: risky black-box technology adds them permanently, while CLEAN ROOM can purge them.

Targeted cards (RECALIBRATE) resolve on a unit you tap after selecting the card — pick the card first, then the unit. With any card selected, the ✕ DISCARD and ♻ RECYCLE buttons in the pile bar let you toss it into the discard pile (ethereal cards burn instead) or tear it out of your deck permanently for a 50% matter refund.

Keywords: EXHAUST — removed for the rest of the sector after play · ETHEREAL — exhausts if still unplayed when the turn ends (hand redraw) · RETAIN — not discarded at turn end · INNATE — guaranteed in the sector's opening hand · CONSUME — torn from the deck permanently after one use. When the draw pile empties, the discard pile reshuffles into it. Deploying to a new sector restores exhausted cards and deals a fresh opening hand. Press D or tap the pile counters to audit every pile.

SALVAGE DOCTRINES [M]

LOOT — Harvesters fire normally; every wreck drops base salvage.
CAPTURE — Harvesters lock a tractor beam on units inside the teal 30% zone shown on every hull bar. The beam channels just 0.85s, slows the victim 55%, and rasps at 35% of Harvester DPS. While a target is beamed, allied units hold fire on it whenever another target is in range, and the beam tolerates integrity drift up to 40%. Completed captures yield ×2.5 salvage, +0.4 grid capacity and bonus Silicon from Titans; escapes or forced kills fail the capture.

UNIT MODULES

MODULE cards bolt a permanent upgrade onto one deployed unit: select the card, then tap a compatible unit on the field (or pick the unit and press INSTALL in the unit panel). One module of a kind per unit; modules exhaust after use, so each card copy installs once per sector. Examples: FLAMETHROWER HEAD makes Needle rounds ignite hosts (40% of each shot burns over 2.5s) · TESLA FRAME lets arcs chain two extra full-strength jumps · SMELTER BELLY boosts a Foundry's output and makes its aura grant damage · RAILGUN COIL makes Rail ignore armor entirely · CRYO GRID deepens the Aegis slow field · HYDRAULIC GRASP speeds up Harvester captures · HOLLOW-POINT AMMO lets Needles punch armor · FRAG SHELLS widens Mortar splash · STATIC GRID makes the Aegis field zap hosts · RANGEFINDER SCOPE / OVERVOLT CAPACITOR stretch range and fire rate on any gun turret.

FIRE DOCTRINES [T]

Every combat unit carries its own targeting order: 1ST furthest along the route · LST closest to your gate · MAX strongest hull · MIN weakest hull · NEAR / FAR by distance from the unit. The active doctrine is badged above each unit — select one and use the chips in the unit panel or T to cycle. E cycles selection through deployed units, right-click cancels a placement.

UNIT INTEGRITY

Units carry their own hull: 20 + 10 per level (boosted by ARMORED MOUNTS and SHIELD GRID). Shrieker blasts and stray meteors chew it away; zero integrity scraps the unit for a 50% refund. WELD now splashes +4 integrity onto every unit, FIELD PATCH restores all, upgrades heal the level-up delta, and the SELF-HEALING HULL relic ticks +1/s. Veterans: units track kills — every 10 kills earns +2% damage (V1–V5 badge, max +10%).

FIELD ABILITIES & WEATHER

SURGE (40 Fe, 45s recharge): every unit fires 50% faster for 8s — golden overdrive frames show who's boosted. WELD (30 Fe 15 Cu, 60s): +3 core integrity and +4 unit integrity on every deployed unit. Waves can roll weather: ION STORM +40% arc damage · GRAV SHEAR hostiles −15% speed · RUST WIND foundries +40% · SOLAR FLARE all units +15% damage · HARD FROST hostiles −10% speed (frost frames) · METEOR SHOWER sky-fire strikes random hostiles (rare stray hits on your units) · SILICON RAIN +30% silicon salvage. Units calibrate every 5 levels (★), gaining +5% damage and rate per star.
